02 / THE STORY

A life beyond
the spotlight.

From a young boy in Gary, Indiana, to a global cultural phenomenon. Discover the journey behind the legend.

1958 — 1969

The beginning
of a legend.

Born on August 29, 1958, in Gary, Indiana, Michael Joseph Jackson showed extraordinary musical talent from an early age.

As the lead singer of the Jackson 5, he became known for his remarkable voice, stage presence and natural ability to captivate audiences.

CHAPTER I
1971 — 1979

Finding his
own universe.

Michael began establishing himself as a solo artist while still performing with his brothers.

The release of Off the Wall in 1979 marked a defining moment, blending disco, funk, soul and pop into a distinctive sound.

CHAPTER II
1982 — 1984

The world
changed forever.

Thriller became a landmark in music history. Its extraordinary success elevated Michael Jackson into an unprecedented global phenomenon.

The album's music videos, including Thriller, Billie Jean and Beat It, helped redefine the visual language of popular music.

CHAPTER III
1987 — 1990

The era of
the icon.

Bad established another extraordinary chapter. With a powerful visual identity and an ambitious world tour, Michael continued to redefine international entertainment.

His influence on choreography, fashion and music videos became unmistakable.

CHAPTER IV
1991 — 1997

Art without
boundaries.

Dangerous and HIStory explored new sounds, visual concepts and ambitious performances.

Through powerful music videos and spectacular tours, Michael continued to expand his artistic language and global reach.

CHAPTER V
2001 — 2009

The final
chapter.

Invincible arrived in 2001, marking his final studio album released during his lifetime.

In 2009, Michael announced This Is It, a series of concerts that was set to mark his return to the stage.
